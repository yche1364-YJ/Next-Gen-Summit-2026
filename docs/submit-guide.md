# Submit your game · Step by step

[← Back to the 3 steps](../README.md)

When your game is finished, upload it so it appears in the **[Game Gallery](https://yche1364-yj.github.io/Next-Gen-Summit-2026/)**. On presentation day, we play everyone's games from there.

**Each team uploads once (not each member). You don't need an account.**

---

## 1. Download your game as a ZIP

1. Open your game in the **Godot web editor** (https://editor.godotengine.org/releases/latest/).
2. **Test it one last time:** press **F5**. What you see is what everyone will play.
3. Stop the game and go back to the **Editor** tab.
4. Click **Project → Tools → Download Project Source**.
5. A ZIP file is saved to your computer, usually in your **Downloads** folder.

⚠️ **Don't unzip it.** Upload the ZIP exactly as it is (smaller than **20 MB**).

## 2. Fill in the upload page

👉 **[Upload your game](https://yche1364-yj.github.io/Next-Gen-Summit-2026/upload.html)**

| Box | What to write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Team name + number** | Your team's name and number | `Team 1` |
| **Mentor name** | Choose your mentor from the list | — |
| **Member full name** 🔒 | Each member's full name. **Private**: only for microcredentials, never posted online | `Alex Chen` |
| **Member nickname** | A fun name for each member. **Public**: shown in the Gallery, so **not your real name** | `PixelFox` |
| **Game title** | Your game's name | `Super Bouncy Adventure` |
| **Describe your game in one sentence** | What's the goal? | `Bounce off the walls to reach the secret coin!` |
| **Controls** | How to play (already filled in; change it if you added keys) | `Player One: ← → to move, ↑ to jump` |
| **Game ZIP file** | Click the box and choose your ZIP (or drag it in) | — |
| **Showcase** (checkbox) | Tick it if you'd like your game playtested on the big screen. It's then listed in the **Playtesting** section of the Gallery. | — |

> **Made a new scene?** The Gallery plays what you see when you press **F5**. If F5 doesn't start your game, right-click your scene in the **FileSystem** panel → **Set as Main Scene**, press **F5** to check, then download the ZIP again.

## 3. Upload

Click **Upload my game** and **keep the page open** until you see **🎉 Uploaded!** It can take up to a minute.

Your teacher checks each game. After that, it appears in the **[Game Gallery](https://yche1364-yj.github.io/Next-Gen-Summit-2026/)**, usually within a few minutes.

---

## Changed your game? Upload it again

Upload the new ZIP, then **tell your teacher**, so they can remove your old version from the Gallery.

---

## Help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **"Wrong class code"** | Type the class code your teacher gives you in the **Class code** box (capital letters don't matter). |
| **"Please fill in the boxes marked in red"** | Fill in every box with a red frame. |
| **"Please use a nickname, not your real name"** | Change the nickname so it isn't your first, last or full name. |
| **"That file is not a ZIP"** | Use **Project → Tools → Download Project Source** in Godot, and don't unzip the file. |
| **"The ZIP file is too big"** | Remove big pictures or sounds you don't use, download the project again, and try again. |
| **"Uploads aren't open yet"** | Your teacher hasn't opened uploads yet. Ask your teacher. |
| I can't find my ZIP | Look in your **Downloads** folder. The name ends with `.zip`. |
| My game isn't in the Gallery yet | Your teacher still needs to check it. Ask your teacher. |
| My game looks different in the Gallery | Press **F5** in Godot. If that's not your game, right-click your scene → **Set as Main Scene**, then download the ZIP and upload again. |

Is the upload page not working at all? Give your ZIP to your teacher, together with your team name + number, mentor, full names, nicknames, game title and one sentence about your game. Your teacher can submit it for you.
